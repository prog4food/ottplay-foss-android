# Code of Conduct

Participants must be respectful, considerate and constructive regardless of
background, identity or experience. Discuss technical evidence, welcome
questions, acknowledge mistakes and respect privacy.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks,
trolling and disclosure of another person's private information are prohibited.
These expectations apply to the project's repositories, issues, pull requests
and official project communications.

Report concerns privately to **alvit.work@gmail.com**, with links and context.
Do not publish sensitive details. The primary maintainer handles reports
confidentially as far as practical, considers the parties' accounts and may
remove content, issue a warning or restrict participation proportionately.
Retaliation is prohibited. A reporter or affected participant may request
reconsideration through the same private channel with additional evidence.

There is no independent conduct moderator. A complaint involving the maintainer
cannot be promised independent review; GitHub's platform reporting facilities
remain available for violations of its policies.
