Plex

Open your personal Plex libraries directly from this player. Sign in with your Plex account and choose a server, or enter its address and access token in Plex settings.

Browse libraries, search, and play videos or music without importing them as TV channels. Automatic playback uses the original file when the device supports it and compatible HLS when needed.

The selected server and token belong to this device's profile. Other providers and their playlists are preserved.
