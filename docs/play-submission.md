# Play Store distribution

Play Store AAB creation and publication belong to the independent
[ottplay-android](https://github.com/open-ott-play/ottplay-android) project.

`ottplay-foss` publishes a signed Full Capacitor APK for sideloading, alongside
its web, desktop and iOS releases. See [Android distributions](android-distributions.md).
The Full signing certificate is separate from the Play upload key.
